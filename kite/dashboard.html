STRATEGY CALCULATIONS
ADXAverage Directional Index

Measures 5-minute trend strength. The strategy requires ADX(14) above 22 and rising.

RSIRelative Strength Index

Momentum filter evaluated on 1-hour and daily candles using period 14.

MACDMoving Average Convergence Divergence

15-minute MACD(12,26,9) line compared with its signal line.

HAHeikin-Ashi EMA

EMA(5) and EMA(9) calculated from 15-minute Heikin-Ashi closes.

OBVOn-Balance Volume

5-minute cumulative volume direction compared with its EMA(5).

ATRAverage True Range

The latest 10-candle high-low span must remain below five times 5-minute ATR(14).

BODYCandle body strength

Absolute body divided by full high-low range: above 60% bullish or 55% bearish.

1DDaily trend filters

Current daily close versus EMA(9), EMA(50), plus daily RSI(14).
